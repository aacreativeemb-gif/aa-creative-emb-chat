# Knowledge Base — AA Creative Emb

## About
AA Creative Emb is an embroidery digitizing and vector art conversion company based in Bradford, UK, serving clients in the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia. The team converts logos and artwork into clean, production-ready embroidery stitch files, and also offers raster-to-vector art conversion for screen printing and other uses.

## What's included with every order

## Supported file formats
DST, PES, EMB, EXP, JEF, CND, EPS, ESS, and more — compatible with all major embroidery machine brands (Tajima, Brother, Janome, Happy, Barudan, Bernina, Husqvarna, Singer, and others).

## How to order
1. Customer submits artwork (JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, or EPS accepted).
2. Customer specifies fabric type, placement, size, and required machine format.
3. AA Creative Emb digitizes the design and runs a quality/stitch simulation check.
4. File is delivered by email, typically within 2–6 hours.
Orders can be submitted via the customer portal at portal.aacreativeemb.com.
